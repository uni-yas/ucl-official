Lattices & sensor placement / 4.6.12 grid

How sparse can sensors be where three different polygons meet?

Improved upper and lower bounds on the density of identifying codes in the truncated trihexagonal (4.6.12) grid

Yasuhito Tsujimura · Research manuscript

Read PDF ↗CiteVerification materials
OverviewTechnical detailsScope of the resultCitationMaterialsResearch enquiries

Overview

At every vertex, a square, a hexagon and a dodecagon meet. We construct a repeating placement with 135 sensors per 312 vertices and also raise the lower bound that every identifying placement must satisfy.

Technical details

We prove 1829/4263 ≤ d ≤ 45/104 on the plane. A radius-22 surplus obstruction supplies the lower bound. In two specified families, 45/104 is the exact minimum.

Scope of the result

One restricted family allows nonperiodic fillings between prescribed anchor columns. The unrestricted minimum remains open.
