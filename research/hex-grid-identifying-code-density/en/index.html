Lattices & sensor placement / Hexagonal grid

Can the most efficient local pattern extend across the plane?

An improved lower bound for identifying codes in the hexagonal grid via a strict local-to-global relaxation gap

Yasuhito Tsujimura · Research manuscript

Read PDF ↗CiteVerification materials
OverviewTechnical detailsScope of the resultCitationMaterialsResearch enquiries

Overview

Imagine identifying every vertex of a honeycomb from the sensors in its immediate neighborhood. A seven-face model reaches density 18/43. Even with every pattern available to an optimal local solution, these patterns cannot fit together across the whole grid.

Technical details

We solve a linear relaxation with consistent one-face marginals and identify all 1,206 oriented stars used by its optimal distributions. A separate radius-15 obstruction yields the universal lower asymptotic density bound 18/43 + 1/79310.

Scope of the result

The bound includes nonperiodic codes. The exact plane minimum remains open.
