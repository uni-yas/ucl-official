An improved lower bound for error-detecting open-locating-dominating sets in the king grid

Yasuhito Tsujimura

Universion Closure Logic · ORCID ↗

Public version: 2026-11-05Version 1.0.0English

Read full paper (PDF)Paper & verification record ↗Cite this paper

Abstract

We prove that every error-detecting open-locating-dominating set in the infinite king grid has lower limiting density at least 79/192 at every center. Together with the construction of Jean and Seo, this gives 79/192 ≤ d_DET:OLD ≤ 13/30, improving their lower bound 3/8. The error model allows at most one false-negative detector report and requires distinguishing all vertices, including the detectors themselves. A fixed integer discharging table assigns a corrected charge of at least 790000 to every vertex when each detector initially carries 1920000 units. This pointwise inequality is verified by exhaustive enumeration of the 2²⁵ assignments on a 5 × 5 square. Exact cancellation on interior edges gives a uniform finite-square estimate, which implies the density bound without assuming periodicity or existence of a density limit. The verification uses no optimization solver. The exact minimum density remains open.

What this result provides

The lower bound 79/192 for error-detecting open-locating-dominating density in the king grid.

Scope

At most one false-negative report; all vertices must be distinguished. The exact density remains open.
