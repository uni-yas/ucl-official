An improved lower bound for error-detecting locating-dominating sets in the square grid

Yasuhito Tsujimura

Universion Closure Logic · ORCID ↗

Public version: 2026-11-05Version 1.0.0English

Read full paper (PDF)Paper & verification record ↗Cite this paper

Abstract

We prove that every error-detecting locating-dominating set in the infinite square grid has lower limiting density at least 168/295 at every center. Together with the upper bound of Jean and Seo, this gives 168/295 ≤ d_DET:LD ≤ 7/12, improving Slater’s lower bound 12/23. A detector distinguishes a target at its own vertex from a target at a neighboring vertex; at most one nonzero report may be replaced by zero, at an unknown detector. We give a fixed integer discharging certificate with initial charge 37760 per detector and corrected charge at least 21504 at every vertex. Its pointwise inequality is verified over all 2²⁵ assignments on a 5 × 5 square. The observation rule uses the four square-grid neighbors, whereas the auxiliary charge corrections use eight directions. Exact cancellation and explicit boundary estimates give finite-square bounds and the density bound on graph balls, which are diamonds. No periodicity or existence of a density limit is assumed. The fixed certificate can be checked without an optimization solver. The exact minimum density remains open.

What this result provides

The lower bound 168/295 for error-detecting locating-dominating density in the square grid.

Scope

The stated one-false-negative model distinguishes self-reports from neighbor reports. The exact density remains open.
