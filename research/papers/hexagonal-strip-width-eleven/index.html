The exact locating-dominating density of the hexagonal strip of width eleven

Yasuhito Tsujimura

Universion Closure Logic · ORCID ↗

Public version: 2026-11-05Version 1.0.0English

Read full paper (PDF)Paper & verification record ↗Cite this paper

Abstract

We prove that the minimum locating-dominating density of the infinite hexagonal strip of width eleven is exactly 48/143, settling the width-eleven instance of Conjecture 9 of Gomes and Wakabayashi. Every locating-dominating set, without a periodicity assumption, contains at least (48L − 192)/13 selected vertices in every interval of L columns. This uniform estimate implies the same density lower bound for the lower limit over graph balls at every center. An explicit horizontally periodic set has 96 selected vertices in a period of 286 vertices and attains the bound. The lower bound is certified by an integer potential on 4,567,987 residual states. A verifier reconstructs these states and all their two-column transitions directly from the strip coordinates, then checks the fixed potential without solving an optimization problem.

What this result provides

The exact width-eleven locating-dominating density 48/143, with an attaining construction and a finite lower-bound certificate.

Scope

The lower bound includes nonperiodic sets. This settles the width-eleven instance, not the general strip-width conjecture.
