Band-sum corrections and finite-mesh evaluation of the Ono–Shiozaki invariants

Yasuhito Tsujimura

Universion Closure Logic · ORCID ↗

Public version: 2026-11-27Version 1.1.0English

Read full paper (PDF)Paper & verification record ↗Cite this paper

Abstract

Ono and Shiozaki conjectured that the bilinear band-sum correction to their gauge-independent mod-two invariants is symmetric. We prove their Conjecture 1 by factoring the exchange defect through the residual gauge differential. Compatibility and antiunitary representation constraints then give the symmetry required by their quadratic refinement. We also give a finite-mesh evaluation of their AI one-cell transition signs from arbitrary sampled Bloch frames. With the source’s factor-system, restriction and seam conventions fixed, symmetry-averaged overlaps yield exactly the source signs on an admissible mesh. Their orientation can be evaluated without polar decomposition, using reduced determinants of the unnormalized products. We make the endpoint comparison and the model holonomy at permuted seams explicit. Two elementary models illustrate sign loss in the full determinant and the necessity of label exchange. The proofs are analytic; exact-arithmetic checks accompany the finite examples. The results concern the specified one-skeleton invariants, not a complete classification of symmetry matrices or higher-cell invariants.

What this result provides

A proof of the source’s band-sum symmetry conjecture and a finite-mesh evaluation of its AI one-cell transition signs.

Scope

The electronic-band construction and conventions of Ono–Shiozaki, with admissibility throughout each mesh interval. AI denotes a symmetry class. No certification from arbitrary samples alone is claimed.
