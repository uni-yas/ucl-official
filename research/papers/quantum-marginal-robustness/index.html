Sharp robustness of quantum marginal reconstruction under composition

Yasuhito Tsujimura

Universion Closure Logic · ORCID ↗

Public version: 2026-11-11Version 1.0.0English

Read full paper (PDF)Paper & verification record ↗Cite this paper

Abstract

Uniqueness from local marginals does not determine how strongly local errors control a quantum state. We study the optimal trace-norm robustness exponent under composition and enlargement of the observed subsystems. For a fixed twelve-qubit product target, with all 66 two-qubit marginals observed, we prove that the sharp exponent is 1/2 for states separable across the two six-qubit blocks and 1/4 for arbitrary states. The latter obstruction is witnessed by pure states of Schmidt rank two. We give sufficient structural conditions for this obstruction, extend it to a positive weighted family, and classify a one-parameter slice completely: its exponent is 1/4 except at one parameter where an exact two-local parent Hamiltonian gives exponent 1/2. For the original product target, three-qubit marginals in just one block restore the square-root exponent. Observing triples in both blocks gives the global bound D ≤ 3^(1/4)√η, where η is the specified sum of marginal trace-norm errors. Thus the minimum maximum support size for square-root recovery is three. The results concern deterministic marginal errors and include arbitrary mixed competing states; measurement counts and statistical estimation costs are not inferred.

What this result provides

Sharp robustness exponents for local-to-global reconstruction of a specified twelve-qubit product target.

Scope

Deterministic marginal errors: exponent 1/2 for block-separable competitors and 1/4 for arbitrary competitors. These exponents are not measurement-cost estimates.
