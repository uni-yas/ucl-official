Limits of a single affine threshold on Pauli pair counts under pooled calibration

Yasuhito Tsujimura

Universion Closure Logic · ORCID ↗

Public version: 2026-11-25Version 1.0English

Read full paper (PDF)Paper & verification record ↗Cite this paper

Abstract

Can a weighted sum of Pauli pair counts certify a quantum state when calibration retains only the total number of failed reference trials? We compare tests on the same twelve-qubit record, requiring at most 5% false acceptance for every state outside a full trace-norm tolerance of 0.1 and every admissible readout. At 1.77 × 10¹⁰ independent preparations, any fixed one-sided affine score accepts the specified reference with probability at most 63.31%, even if the pooled count determines its cutoff. A joint test accepts that reference with probability above 98.31%. The separation comes from two stationary readouts with identical pooled calibration laws. Under the second readout, a mixture reproduces the reference mean pair table. The mixture lies within tolerance, yet it decomposes into pure states of the same fidelity that all lie outside. Each score direction admits an adverse component. One additional calibration bit distinguishes the two readouts with error below exp(−4800).

What this result provides

A limitation of every fixed one-sided affine score, compared with a joint test on the same record under pooled calibration.

Scope

The weights are fixed and calibration is deliberately pooled. The reference acceptance bounds concern the stated target, readout model and sample budget; one additional calibration bit distinguishes the constructed readouts.
