The exact open-locating-dominating density of the kagome grid

Yasuhito Tsujimura

Universion Closure Logic · ORCID ↗

Public version: 2026-11-27Version 1.1.0English

Read full paper (PDF)Paper & verification record ↗Cite this paper

Abstract

We determine the minimum density of an open-locating-dominating set in the infinite kagome grid, also called the trihexagonal or hexagon-triangle grid. The value is exactly 2/5. The lower bound is the known degree-four counting bound; our contribution is an explicit periodic set attaining it, with twelve selected vertices in a fundamental region of thirty vertices. We prove the construction valid using a ten-residue table and the fact that two distinct vertices of the kagome grid have at most one common neighbor. The lower bound holds for the lower limiting density of every OLD set at every center, without a periodicity assumption. A supplementary verifier checks the same construction directly in the infinite-grid coordinates, including all comparisons across the period boundary.

What this result provides

An explicit periodic construction attaining the known lower bound 2/5 for the infinite kagome grid.

Scope

Open neighborhoods distinguish all vertices, including selected vertices. The lower bound is prior work; the attaining construction is the contribution.
