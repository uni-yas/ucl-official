Density bounds for identifying codes in the hexagonal and truncated trihexagonal grids

Yasuhito Tsujimura

Universion Closure Logic · ORCID ↗

Public version: 2026-11-11Version 2.0.0English

Read full paper (PDF)Paper & verification record ↗Cite this paper

Abstract

We obtain density bounds for radius-one identifying codes in two infinite 3-regular planar grids. In the hexagonal grid, every code has lower asymptotic density at least 18/43 + 1/79310, improving the lower bound of 23/55. The seven-face marginal relaxation has exact value 18/43. The union of the supports of all optimal distributions consists of exactly 1,206 oriented stars, but no plane identifying code has its centered star in this union at every face. A separate obstruction gives the density increment: every radius-15 face ball contains a face with surplus at least 1/55. In the truncated trihexagonal (4.6.12) grid, we improve both ends of the known interval from [3/7, 13/30] to [1829/4263, 45/104]. Every radius-22 vertex ball has total surplus at least 2 under Sampaio’s discharging rule. A periodic code attains the upper bound, which is optimal among codes invariant under a prescribed translation and also in a class with prescribed anchor columns and arbitrary intervening fillings. Both plane lower bounds hold at every ball center and require no periodicity assumption.

What this result provides

Improved lower bounds for hexagonal and truncated trihexagonal grids, and a periodic upper construction for the latter.

Scope

Radius-one identifying codes. The unrestricted exact densities remain open; optimality of the upper construction is proved only in the stated restricted classes.
