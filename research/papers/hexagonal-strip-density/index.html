Locating-dominating sets in hexagonal strips: minimum density 1/3 exactly at widths divisible by 3

Yasuhito Tsujimura

Universion Closure Logic · ORCID ↗

Public version: 2026-11-11Version 1.1.0English

Read full paper (PDF)Paper & verification record ↗Cite this paper

Abstract

The minimum density of a locating-dominating set in the infinite hexagonal strip of width k equals 1/3 if and only if 3 divides k. Gomes and Wakabayashi proved equality for widths divisible by three. We prove the converse and show that the excess above 1/3 is Θ(k⁻²) as k grows in either of the other two residue classes. The lower bound follows by removing three rows at a time from a boundary with no local defect.

For every width we give an explicit periodic locating-dominating set whose density equals the value proposed in Conjecture 9 of Gomes and Wakabayashi. At widths 7, 8 and 10, we prove matching lower bounds for arbitrary sets satisfying the locating-domination conditions, including nonperiodic sets. The resulting minimum densities are 26/77, 27/80 and 47/140, respectively. The exact formula for general widths outside the multiples of three remains open.

What this result provides

A width classification for density 1/3, explicit constructions, and exact densities at widths 7, 8 and 10.

Scope

Nonperiodic lower bounds are included. The general exact-density formula outside widths divisible by three remains open.
