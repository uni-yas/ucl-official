Triangle-overlap cores and density bounds for Tuza's conjecture

Yasuhito Tsujimura

Universion Closure Logic · ORCID ↗

Public version: 2026-11-10Version 1.0.0English

Abstract

For a finite simple graph G, let ν(G) be the maximum number of edge-disjoint triangles and let τ(G) be the minimum number of edges meeting every triangle. We prove τ(G) ≤ 2ν(G) when each edge belongs to at most four triangles, the edges belonging to four triangles form components with at most two edges, and the maximum average degree is at most 25/2. No upper bound on maximum vertex degree is imposed. We also localize the density condition. Let K be the Hifumi core, defined as the vertex set of the 4-core of the spanning subgraph formed by edges belonging to at least three triangles. It suffices to impose the density condition on G[K], including all original edges between vertices of K. More generally, when the four-triangle edges have maximum degree at most two, reductions produce a subgraph R ⊆ G[K] with τ(G) − 2ν(G) ≤ τ(R) − 2ν(R). Consequently an empty core gives a density-free sufficient condition. For the first graph class, a quantitative surplus argument strengthens both the global and localized density conditions to the strict bound 25/2 + 2/56775. The proof combines edge reductions, discharging, finite certificates and a weak König–Egerváry lemma for links of arbitrary order. The general conjecture and the removal of the density condition from the first graph class remain unresolved here.

What this result provides

A sufficient condition for Tuza’s inequality based on triangle overlap and a localized density bound.

Scope

Finite simple graphs satisfying the stated overlap restrictions. The general Tuza conjecture remains open.
