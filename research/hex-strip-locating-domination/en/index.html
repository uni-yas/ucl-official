Which strip widths need only one sensor per three vertices?

Locating-dominating sets in hexagonal strips: minimum density 1/3 exactly at widths divisible by 3

Yasuhito Tsujimura · Author manuscript

Read PDF ↗CiteVerification materials
OverviewTechnical detailsScope of the resultCitationMaterialsResearch enquiries

Overview

On an infinite honeycomb strip, sensors must distinguish every unselected vertex by its neighbors. The minimum density is exactly one third if and only if the width is divisible by three.

Technical details

At a perfect boundary three rows can be peeled away. In the other two residue classes the excess density is Θ(k⁻²) at width k. For widths 7, 8 and 10 we prove exact densities of 26/77, 27/80 and 47/140.

Scope of the result

Explicit periodic constructions cover every width. The conjectured exact formula is open at widths at least 11 not divisible by three.

Citation

Yasuhito Tsujimura. “Locating-dominating sets in hexagonal strips: minimum density 1/3 exactly at widths divisible by 3.” Manuscript.
