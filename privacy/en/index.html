Website privacy

Website measurement

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to improve this website. It aggregates page views, referrers, device and browser types, and page loading performance.

Analytics without individual tracking

We do not use cookies or localStorage for analytics, or create persistent visitor fingerprints. These measurements are not used for advertising or individual browsing profiles. We may save your language preference in your browser to make the website easier to use.

What we do not send

Names, email addresses and message text entered in the contact form are not sent to analytics. This website does not collect usage data from the QuotaEcho application. New measurement through the previous Google Analytics implementation has been stopped.

External services and contact

Cloudflare processes the website measurements. Contact form submissions are used to respond to inquiries and are delivered through FormSubmit. Hosting and other service providers may process access logs for operations and security.
