Website privacy

Website analytics

With your consent, we use Google Analytics to record page visits, clicks to papers, products and the App Store, and successful contact submissions to improve this website. Data sent to Google also includes cookie identifiers, browser and device information, and approximate location information.

What we do not send

We do not send names, email addresses or message contents entered in our contact form to analytics. We do not use Google Signals or advertising personalization. Website measurement does not collect usage data from the QuotaEcho app.

Your choice

All website features remain available if you decline. Your choice is stored in this browser for 180 days and can be changed using Analytics settings below or in the footer of each page. Withdrawing consent stops further measurement and removes Google Analytics cookies created by this site. It does not delete data already sent.

External services and contact

Analytics data is sent to Google. We use contact form details to respond to your inquiry; FormSubmit handles form delivery. Our hosting provider may process access logs to operate and protect the service.
