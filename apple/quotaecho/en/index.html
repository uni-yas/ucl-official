IN THE APP · ANOTHER EXAMPLE

From a period
back to your work.

See up to five periods with the largest usage changes over the last 7 days (168 hours). Pick one to review its tasks and model settings, then open a task in Codex.

  • Usage is compared at roughly hourly checks. Displayed periods use the actual timestamps recorded by Codex; older records without timestamps use QuotaEcho check times
  • If the recorded interval differs from about an hour, its duration is shown too
  • History stays after reset. Each period must fall entirely within the last 7 days; older periods leave the list as time passes
  • Usage changes calculated separately within each weekly window
  • Processing counts, duration, concurrency, and tool use. A recent median when the selected record interval is roughly an hour and at least three other periods are comparable
  • Automatic checks while the display is off or locked; missed periods reconstructed where the remaining usage records allow

Codex, Work and other supported services share the allowance. QuotaEcho displays usage recorded by Codex on your Mac, alongside tasks and processing activity available on that same Mac. Use them to review changes alongside the work happening at the time. They do not establish per-task consumption or the cause of a change.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Scope

This policy describes how Universion Closure Logic, K.K. (“we,” “us,” or “the Company”) handles information in the macOS app QuotaEcho.

Information used by the app

After you grant read-only access in macOS, QuotaEcho extracts metadata from Codex records, including the weekly allowance, task names, processing times and counts, and recorded model and reasoning settings. This information is processed on your Mac and is not transmitted to us by the app.

Information we do not obtain

QuotaEcho does not obtain your OpenAI account ID, password, API key, cookies, authentication tokens, or auth.json. It does not extract, store, display, or transmit prompts, response bodies, reasoning, code, file paths, commands, or command output.

Information stored on your Mac

QuotaEcho stores the current weekly allowance and the usage history needed to compare the last 7 days, language, appearance and sound preferences, read-only permission information, a local matching value used to renew that permission safely for the same records folder, and a randomly generated local identifier inside the app sandbox. Task names, model settings and processing summaries are used in memory only while displayed and are not retained in the usage history.

Retention and deletion

History needed to review the last 7 days is kept after the weekly allowance resets. Records from past weekly windows that are no longer part of that comparison are pruned at startup and while the app runs. The last confirmed allowance record is kept until a valid new weekly allowance is obtained. You can remove QuotaEcho’s saved history, settings and read permission through the uninstall instructions in Help. Codex tasks and records are kept.

Support communications

If you contact support by email, we receive your email address and message content and use them to respond and resolve the issue. Support communications may be processed by our email service provider.

Website

The code for this website does not use advertising or behavioral-tracking cookies or analytics tools. Our hosting provider may temporarily process server logs such as IP address, browser information, and access time for security and reliable operation.

Relationship with third parties

QuotaEcho is provided independently from OpenAI. It does not imply affiliation, sponsorship, endorsement, provision, or warranty by OpenAI. OpenAI’s own service information and terms govern actual availability, allowance, and account conditions.
